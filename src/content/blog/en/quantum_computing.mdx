## Quantum Computing

It's not something you absolutely need to know, but learning new technology is always fun.

Quantum computers.

They might seem like incredible futuristic technology straight out of a sci-fi movie, but they're closer than you think.\
Close enough for us to actually use them. (Physically close, too.)

To do quantum computing, you need to write quantum circuits.\
The tools used for this include IBM's Qiskit and Microsoft's Q#, among others.\
Qiskit is IBM's Python-based open-source SDK that lets you run quantum computing on the IBM Cloud.\
Q# is Microsoft's C#-based QDK (Quantum Development Kit), which provides access to quantum computing through Azure Quantum.

<LinkPreview src="https://github.com/microsoft/qsharp"/>

<LinkPreview src="https://github.com/qiskit/qiskit"/>

While there is plenty of material on Q# as well, IBM provides a truly vast and up-to-date collection of resources on quantum computing and Qiskit.\
So today, let's try using a quantum computer with Qiskit.
