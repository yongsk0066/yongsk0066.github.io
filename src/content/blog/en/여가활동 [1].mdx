I loved the [Midjourney landing page](https://www.midjourney.com/home) so much that I looked into what library they used, and that led me to a cloning video.

<YouTube src="https://youtu.be/YCJgQI71jEE?si=KOp-YtU2n-yCZR8z" />

Looking at their work, the amount of code wasn't that much, so I decided to try cloning it myself.

I extracted just the transform logic and made it pluggable in a pipeline style.
Then I wrapped it all up nicely as a Lit element.

Here's what it can do.

### Basic

<AsciiElement
  transforms={[]}
  />

### Midjourney-style spiral

```tsx
<AsciiElement
  transforms={['spiral']} />
```

<AsciiElement
  transforms={['spiral']}
  />

### spiral -> zoom -> wave

```tsx
<AsciiElement
  transforms={['spiral', 'zoom', 'wave']}/>
```

<AsciiElement
  transforms={['spiral', 'zoom', 'wave']}
  />


### Wait long enough and it forms a galaxy shape

<AsciiElement
  transforms={['hurricane', 'spiral']}
  />
